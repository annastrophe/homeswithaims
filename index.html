The SAFE Upgrading Plan.

Upgrading from your first BTO usually means swapping a small mortgage for a much bigger one. SAFE is the four-part check we run on every upgrade before we recommend it. If a plan fails any part, we rework it or advise against the move.

  1. S

    Sale pays for it

    The proceeds from your current home fund the next one. No cash top-up from your savings.

  2. A

    Aside first

    Before anything goes towards the new home, part of your sale proceeds is set aside as an emergency fund.

  3. F

    Four years covered

    Four years of mortgage payments are covered by your CPF and a cash buffer taken from the sale proceeds.

  4. E

    Even if rates rise

    We test the plan at a higher interest rate than today's, so it still holds if your loan gets more expensive.

The SAFE Upgrading Plan is a planning method used by Homes with AIMS. It is a general guide and not financial advice; your figures depend on your own loan, CPF and income.

Questions we hear often.

What happens in a consultation with Homes with AIMS?

We sit down with you, in person or over a call, and go through four things: what your flat is likely to sell for, how much cash and CPF you would have after the sale, what you can afford next under our SAFE Upgrading Plan, and a timeline for selling and buying. You leave with a written summary of your numbers.

Is the consultation free, and do I have to engage you after?

The consultation is free and there is no obligation to list or buy with us. If the numbers say you should stay where you are, we will tell you that.

My flat just reached MOP. Should I sell now?

It depends on your next home, your loan and CPF position, and how many similar units are listed nearby. We run the numbers with you before you decide.

Can I sell and buy without moving out twice?

Usually, yes. We sequence the sale and purchase and, where it fits, arrange a temporary extension of stay so you move straight into your next home.

Do you handle inherited flats, faraid and divorce cases?

Yes. We regularly handle sales that involve an estate, faraid distribution or a divorce. Your lawyer handles the legal side, and we work alongside them on the sale, the timeline and the paperwork with HDB.

Do you work outside Punggol and Sengkang?

Punggol and Sengkang are our focus, but if you are moving out of the northeast, or into it from elsewhere, we handle both ends.

I only want to know what flats in my block sold for. Do I need a consultation?

No. Use our free sale check at sell.homeswithaims.com and we will WhatsApp you the recent sales in your block.
