Questions we hear often

My flat just reached MOP. Should I sell now? +

It depends on your next home, your loan and CPF position, and how many similar units are listed nearby. We'll run the numbers with you before you decide — no commitment.

Can I sell and buy without moving out twice? +

Usually, yes. We sequence the sale and purchase, and where it fits, arrange an extension of stay so you move straight into your next home.

How is your valuation worked out? +

We look at recent transactions in your block and neighbouring blocks, then adjust for floor, facing, condition and remaining lease — and explain each step.

Do you work outside Punggol and Sengkang? +

These two towns are our focus, but if you're moving out of — or into — the northeast from elsewhere, we'll handle both ends.
